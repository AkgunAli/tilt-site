Privacy Policy

Tilt: Gravity Puzzle · Last updated 16 November 2026

Tilt is a puzzle game. It has no accounts, no ads and no analytics, and it does not track you across apps or websites. This page explains the little data the game uses.

On your device

Your progress (levels, stars, daily streak) and settings (language, sound, vibration) are stored only on your device. "Reset progress" in Settings deletes them.

Leaderboards

To show leaderboards, the game sends the following to our server when you finish a level or the daily puzzle, or open the leaderboard:

  • a random player ID and a random secret created by the app on your device (they are not linked to your Apple ID, name, email, phone number or device identifiers);
  • the turns you made in that puzzle, so the server can check the result, and the resulting number of turns, stars and levels cleared;
  • the time of the result.

The server picks a display name for you from a fixed list of words (for example "Swift Otter 42"); you cannot enter your own text. Other players see this name and your results on the leaderboards. We do not collect your IP address in our database; the hosting provider may keep short-lived technical logs as part of running the service.

Leaderboard data is stored with Supabase, our hosting provider, and is used only to run the leaderboards. It is not sold or shared with anyone else.

Deleting your leaderboard data

Because there is no account, we cannot tell who you are from the data. To have your leaderboard entries removed, send a request with the name shown on your leaderboard, and we will delete it. Deleting the app removes the random ID from your device; old entries then stay anonymous.

Children

The game does not ask for or knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy changes (for example if ads are added), we will update this page and the date above before the change takes effect.

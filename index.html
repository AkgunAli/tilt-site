Tilt: Gravity Puzzle

Turn the box. Clear the tiles.

How to play

Swipe the box, or tap the turn buttons, to turn it a quarter. Tiles fall. Three or more touching tiles of the same colour vanish. Empty the box in as few turns as you can: reach the target for three stars. Stuck? Tap Hint to see the best next turn.

Special tiles

  • Grey stones never vanish by matching. You only need to clear the coloured tiles.
  • Frosted tiles need two matches: the first one breaks the ice.
  • Tiles with corner pins never fall. Other tiles land on them.
  • Spiked bombs, when matched, clear every tile around them, stones too.

Leaderboards

The daily puzzle is the same box for everyone and changes at midnight UTC. The leaderboard shows who emptied it in the fewest turns. The levels leaderboard counts levels cleared, then stars. Your name is chosen for you; tap New name on the leaderboard to get another one.
